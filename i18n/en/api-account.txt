# The JSON API's account settings (crates/api/src/account.rs) and the phone app's devices
# (devices.rs): what they say, in the language each request asks for (crates/api/src/lang.rs),
# where the pages' own words (account.txt) don't say it: a name, a contact taken or kept, the word
# that closes the account it says as "Cilësimet" does; a device's platform and token.

# crates/api/src/account.rs: a language the site doesn't speak, under "lang"

Duhet të jetë „sq“ ose „en“.
= Must be “sq” or “en”.

# crates/api/src/account.rs: the last contact, which signing in needs, under "kind"

Llogaria jote duhet të ketë të paktën një numër ose email, që të hysh me të.
= Your account needs at least one number or email to sign in with.

# crates/api/src/account.rs: closing the account without the word that says it is meant, under
# "confirm" (FSHI, in English DELETE, as the page's box asks for it)

Shkruaj FSHI, që të dimë se e do vërtet.
= Type DELETE so we know you mean it.

# crates/api/src/devices.rs: a device's platform that is neither the app's, under "platform"

Duhet të jetë „ios“ ose „android“.
= Must be “ios” or “android”.

# crates/api/src/devices.rs: a device's token empty or too long, under "token" (TOKEN_MOST)

Tokeni i pajisjes duhet të ketë 1 deri në {} shenja.
= The device’s token needs 1 to {} characters.
