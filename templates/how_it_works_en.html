Pluribus is a classifieds site for Albania: cars, property, electronics, jobs, services and everything else, from people and businesses near you. Searching and posting listings are free.

Search

Type what you’re looking for in the search box, e.g. “Golf 7” or “flat for rent in Tirana”. Words that name a category, a town, a sale or a rental become filters, in English as in Albanian (“apartament me qira në Tiranë”), which you take off with a tap if you didn’t mean them; the search forgives typos and finds the other forms of a word. On a category’s page, “Filters” narrow the list by what matters there (make and year, rooms and floor area, model…), “Map” shows the listings around a point, and the town you choose is remembered for the other pages.

Save and follow

The heart on a listing’s photo saves it to “Saved” (you need an account). When a saved listing’s price drops, the heart at the top of the page gets a dot and the listing says by how much.

On a category’s page or a search’s, “Save search” keeps it, with the filters you’ve chosen, in “Saved searches”. There you see how many new listings have come up since you last opened it, and the heart gets a dot when they do; if you sign in with email, you also get an email, no more than one every three hours for each search.

Contact the seller

On the listing’s page, the phone button shows the seller’s number (or email) when you press it; the message button opens a conversation within Pluribus, which you’ll find in “Messages”. Pluribus isn’t a party to the sale: the price, delivery and payment are agreed between you and the seller, and the safety tips apply to both sides.

Post a listing

Press “+” at the top. You sign in with your mobile number or your email: we send you a 6-digit code, no password. Write the title and the description, upload up to 8 photos (the first one is the cover), choose the category and fill in its details; the form can fill them in by itself from the title, the description and the photos (“Fill in the rest with AI”), and you check them before you publish. The listing goes up straight away; only one with the same photos as another seller’s listing waits for a moderator to look at it.

Your listings

“My listings” shows how many times each listing was viewed, how many times someone took your number and how many times it was saved. There you can edit a listing, mark it reserved or sold (a rental: rented out), pause it for a while or delete it. A sold listing keeps its link, saying that it was sold, but it drops out of the lists and no one can start a conversation about it any more. A listing stays on the site for 60 days: three days before it expires we let you know, and “Renew” keeps it up for another 60 days, or puts it back up after it has expired. Renewing also takes it back to the top of the newest listings, no more than once a week. In “Settings” you change your name, number or email, choose what we notify you about and delete your account. Your number is shown only when someone presses the button to see it.

Trust

A seller who has signed in with the code sent to their number has the “Verified number” badge on their listings and profile. Every listing has “Report”: reports for serious reasons take it off the site until a moderator has looked at it, and the moderator can also suspend the seller’s account. To stop seeing a seller, block them after reporting their listing, or from your conversation: their listings disappear for you and neither of you can write to the other.
