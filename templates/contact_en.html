Quicker than an email

  • A suspicious or wrong listing: press “Report” on its page. The moderators look at it, and many reports take the listing down at once.
  • A seller you don’t want to see again: “Block seller” after reporting their listing, or in your conversation.
  • A listing of yours: edit, pause or delete it in “My listings”.
